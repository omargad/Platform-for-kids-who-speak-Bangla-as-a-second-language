# Bangla Adventures — First Client Meeting

**Proposed duration:** 45 minutes  
**Client:** K. M. Abul Faysal Ibn Alam  
**Project team:** Bahaa and team  
**Purpose:** align on the child audience, learning and cultural outcomes, MVP scope, governance, delivery constraints, and next actions

## Pre-read to send 24 hours before

- Private Bangla Adventures review link
- `PROJECT_PACK.md`, especially sections 1, 6, 13, and 15
- One request: please identify any existing content, brand material, cloud environment, or mandatory launch constraint

## Agenda

### 0–5 minutes — Introductions and desired outcome

- Roles, decision makers, and project communication channel
- Client’s reason for proposing the project
- What would make the meeting successful today?

**Decision:** named client product owner and project-team contact.

### 5–12 minutes — Vision and audience

- Primary age band
- Target countries, family contexts, and community-school use
- Starting Bangla level: speaking, listening, reading, and writing
- Product’s intended emotional outcome: confidence, identity, curiosity, family connection, curriculum support

**Decision:** one primary learner segment and one-sentence product outcome.

### 12–20 minutes — Prototype walkthrough

- Quest-map first screen
- Six-band Pre-A1 to C1–C2 learning pathway and starting-level guide
- One complete lesson: goals, vocabulary audio, explicit teaching, guided practice, video/playlist, and check
- Word audio and practice cards
- Bilingual river story and question
- UNESCO culture passport
- Matching game and device-local progress
- Grown-ups privacy panel

Ask the client to mark each as **must have**, **should have**, **later**, or **remove**.

**Decision:** agreed prototype strengths and top five changes.

### 20–28 minutes — Content and cultural governance

- Standard Bangla, dialect treatment, transliteration, and pronunciation
- Content balance: language, history, geography, everyday life, arts, nature, faiths, festivals, food
- Named language and cultural reviewers
- Source, copyright, illustration, and audio approval process

**Decision:** content owner, reviewers, and approval workflow.

### 28–35 minutes — Safety, privacy, and accessibility

- Confirm anonymous child journey for the MVP
- Adult or teacher needs that might require accounts
- Australian Children’s Online Privacy Code readiness
- Child testing, consent, safeguarding, and complaints/escalation
- WCAG 2.2 AA target and disabled-user testing

**Decision:** privacy, safeguarding, and accessibility owners; any account feature deferred until assessment.

### 35–40 minutes — Technical and operational constraints

- Cloud provider and account-access process
- Domain, hosting, repository, environments, and ownership
- Supported devices, browsers, networks, offline requirement
- Budget for professional audio, content review, accessibility, and testing

**Decision:** technical owner and environment-selection process.

### 40–45 minutes — Scope and next actions

- Confirm MVP boundary and proposed 12-week roadmap
- Identify pilot users and recruitment/consent route
- Agree weekly meeting time and decision process
- Assign actions and dates

**Decision:** next milestone, owners, and date.

## Priority question bank

1. Which single child group must the MVP serve exceptionally well?
2. What should that child be able to understand, say, read, or feel after four weeks?
3. Who has final authority over Bangla and cultural accuracy?
4. Are human voice recordings available or funded?
5. Is home learning, classroom use, or both the priority?
6. What is the minimum content volume for the first pilot?
7. Can the MVP remain anonymous with device-local progress?
8. Which user need would justify an adult or teacher account?
9. Which target-country privacy or school requirements apply beyond Australia?
10. Who can recruit families and educators for consented testing?
11. What cloud and repository standards already exist at the client?
12. Which deadline, budget, or academic assessment constraint is immovable?
