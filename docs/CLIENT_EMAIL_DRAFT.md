Dear Faysal,

I hope you are well.

We are Bahaa and the Swinburne project team assigned to the “Platform for kids who speak Bangla as a second language” project. We are excited to work with you on an interactive experience that helps children living outside Bangladesh learn Bangla while exploring the country’s history, places, and culture.

We have completed the initial research and built a private, review-ready learning platform for discussion. It now includes 18 complete lessons across six child- and heritage-adapted proficiency bands from Pre-A1 through a C1–C2 extension. Every lesson has learning goals, vocabulary and model sentences with Bangla audio, explicit teaching, cultural context, guided practice, a family activity, a knowledge check, a relevant YouTube video, and a curated playlist. The platform also includes a starting-level guide, bilingual English/Bangla navigation, a river story, UNESCO culture postcards, a matching game, and device-local progress. We have framed privacy, accessibility, cultural review, media governance, and child-safety requirements for a public release.

Could we arrange a 45-minute introductory meeting? We would like to use it to:

- understand your vision and preferred learner age group;
- confirm the Bangla language, pronunciation, and cultural review approach;
- walk through the full curriculum and agree on review and launch priorities;
- discuss privacy, accessibility, hosting, domain, and cloud support; and
- agree on the roadmap, responsibilities, and next milestone.

Please let us know a few suitable times over the next week, along with your preferred meeting platform and time zone. We are based in Melbourne and can coordinate around your availability.

If helpful, we can send the prototype and a short pre-read before the meeting.
