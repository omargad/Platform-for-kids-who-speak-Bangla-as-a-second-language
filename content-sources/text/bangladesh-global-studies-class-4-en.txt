Many small ethnic groups live in Bangladesh. They have their own lifestyle and
Among these groups, the Chakma are the majority. Most of them live in the
districts of Rangamati, Bandarban, and Khagrachhari. They have their own
language and alphabet, called Changma. They have literature written in their
language. Chakma families are patriarchal, which means the father is the head of
the family and the owner of property. Every village has a leader called Karbari.
The Chakma people also have their own king. They build their houses with
wood and bamboo on raised platforms. They do their farming in a method called
jhum. Most of them are Buddhists. One of their religious festivals is Kathin
Chibar Dan. Their main social festival is Bijhu. During the festivals, they
clothes using the traditional waist-loom. Chakma women wear a dress called
Pinon from the waist to the ankle, and a scarf called Khadi on the upper part of
the body. Chakma men usually wear a dhuti or lungi on the lower body and a
cloth called silum on the upper body. The Chakma culture has both similarities
and dissimilarities with Bangali culture. We will learn Chakma culture and live
in harmony maintaining social relations.
